5 WAYS TO JOIN THIS CAMPAIGN

Join this campaign as a medic or medical association by signing our petition and joining our events around the General Assembly of the World Medical Assocation in Rotterdam (7 - 10 October 2026).

  1. Read and sign our petition
  2. Sign up for our conference on October 8 (Rotterdam)
  3. Participate in our action on October 10 (Rotterdam)
  4. Write your own association (and speak out in other ways)
  5. Or contact us on suspend_ima@proton.me for questions and ideas
