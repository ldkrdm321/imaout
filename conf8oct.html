Speakers

Jeff Handmaker

Jeff Handmaker is Associate Professor at Erasmus University Rotterdam and specialises in human rights and international law. He will address the destruction of healthcare in Palestine and its legal implications under international humanitarian law.

Marcelia Theron

Marcelia Theron is a South African physician and healthcare activist. She is a member of the Global Anti-Apartheid Movement and has worked with Healthcare Workers 4 Palestine South Africa (HCW4P). She will speak in support of the call to suspend the IMA in defence of global standards of medical ethics.

Mvuyisi Mzukwa

Mvuyisi Mzukwa is a general practitioner and Chairperson of the South African Medical Association (SAMA). He will discuss why SAMA broke ties with the Israeli Medical Association and its call for other medical associations to follow suit.

Hanne Bosselaers

Hanne Bosselaers is a general practitioner and member of Medicine for the People, with longstanding involvement in solidarity initiatives for Palestinian healthcare. She will speak about the role and responsibility of the medical community.

Why this meeting?

From 7–10 October 2026, the World Medical Association (WMA) will hold its General Assembly in Rotterdam. The WMA presents itself as the international guardian of medical ethics and is responsible for major ethical standards including the Declaration of Geneva and the Declaration of Tokyo.

The IMA OUT campaign is calling for the suspension of the Israeli Medical Association from the WMA. During this fringe conference, healthcare professionals, academics and activists will discuss medical ethics, medical neutrality, attacks on Palestinian healthcare and the responsibilities of medical institutions.

The evening will move from evidence to action: from the destruction of healthcare and the relevant ethical and legal principles to the question of what medical associations and healthcare professionals can do.
