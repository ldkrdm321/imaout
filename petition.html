Uphold Medical Ethics;
Suspend the Israeli Medical Association from the World Medical Association

From 7–10 October 2026, the World Medical Association (WMA) will hold its General Assembly in Rotterdam, The Netherlands. The main role of the WMA is to establish and promote the highest international standards of medical ethics, professional conduct, and patient care for doctors.

We call on the WMA and its member associations to act now and suspend the Israeli Medical Association (IMA), because the IMA has failed to uphold core medical ethical standards, including the WMA Declarations of Geneva and Tokyo. The IMA is also complicit in serious violations of medical neutrality and human rights, committed by the Israeli military and government. This situation has been going on for decades and the WMA has failed to date, to act on these fundamentally important issues. This Assembly is a critical moment for the international medical community to act.

Why this action is needed

There is abundant evidence from Palestinian health workers, international human rights bodies, and academic research documenting:

  • Attacks on healthcare in Palestine by Israeli forces. Palestinian, Lebanese and Iranian hospitals continue to be destroyed, ambulances targeted, and healthcare workers killed, detained, tortured and obstructed from delivering care.
  • Ongoing blocking of medical and humanitarian aid to Palestine by Israel. Israel restricts the delivery of medicines, equipment, fuel, and humanitarian personnel, preventing access to essential care.
  • Complicity of Israeli medical professionals in torture and inhumane treatment of Palestinians within the Israeli prison system. Credible reports of this going back three decades indicate that abuse in detention settings has not been properly documented, investigated, or opposed by the IMA, which is in violation of fundamental medical ethics.
  • Systemic inequality in health and access to care affecting Palestinians under illegal occupation and apartheid policies enforced by Israel. Palestinians face significantly worse health outcomes, and are provided with worse health services than Israelis, due to structural restrictions caused by the Israeli illegal occupation and its apartheid policies. The IMA has never challenged these inequalities.
  • Genocide in Palestine. The Independent International Commission of Inquiry established by the United Nations Human Rights Council concluded in its September 2025 report that Israeli authorities and security forces had committed genocide in Gaza. Meanwhile, the International Court of Justice has not yet issued a final ruling on the merits of the case; however, it found the claim regarding the right of Palestinians in Gaza to be protected against acts covered by the Genocide Convention to be plausible and ordered binding provisional measures against Israel.
  • The Palestinian people have asked for our solidarity: they have called for a boycott and international isolation of all complicit Israeli institutions and organisations, just as happened during apartheid in South Africa in the 1980’s. We have a duty to listen to them.

We call on the member national medical associations of the WMA to:

  • Cut your institutional ties with the Israeli Medical Association, in line with the decision of the South African Medical Association, the British Medical Association and the Turkish Medical Association.
  • Advocate within the WMA for the suspension of the IMA for its multiple violations of medical ethics and international humanitarian law, including its failure to speak out against the genocide in Palestine, the destruction of healthcare infrastructure and the torture and killing of healthcare workers.
  • If WMA leadership fails to take meaningful action and prevents debate at the Rotterdam conference about this issue, we ask you to consider boycotting the WMA and withholding your membership fees to the WMA from 2027, until it does take action.

We call on the World Medical Association to:

  • Suspend the Israeli Medical Association (IMA), until it publicly upholds medical ethics and humanitarian law, condemns murder and torture of Palestinian health workers and takes action against violations by its members, as set out in your Porto resolution of 13 October 2025.
  • Ensure accountability within the global medical profession: undertake investigations of breaches of ethics and humanitarian law by national medical associations, and enforce disciplinary measures.

Why this matters

Medical ethics are the foundation of trust in healthcare. The international medical community has acted before, including during apartheid in South Africa. If medical ethics are to mean anything in the future, and we are to have confidence that the WMA can fulfil its role as the international guardian of medical ethics, then it must take concerted action to hold the IMA to account in Rotterdam this October.

Silence is not neutral. Inaction has consequences. As Archbishop Desmond Tutu famously said, “If you are neutral in situations of injustice, you have chosen the side of the oppressor.”
